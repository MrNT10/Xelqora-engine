About the Project

Built for experimentation and growth

Xelqora Engine is intended to grow from a concept into a real engine platform. The project is currently positioned as an early-stage open-source initiative with room to expand into rendering, tools, games, and public demos.

Technology Direction

What Xelqora aims to explore

These are development goals and focus areas, not promises of completed features. The site presents the vision honestly while still giving the project a polished identity.

RG

Xelqora Renderer

Real-time rendering for interactive scenes with a browser-friendly pipeline as the early focus.

XS

Xelqora Super Resolution

An original upscale concept aimed at improving performance while preserving image quality.

XN

Xelqora Neural Reconstruction

Longer-term research into AI-assisted reconstruction and advanced image refinement techniques.

XP

Xelqora Physics

Movement, collisions, gravity, bounce behaviours, and gameplay interactions for demos and games.

XA

Xelqora Audio

Playback systems, sound effects, music support, and future engine-side audio tools.

LG

Xelqora Legacy

Compatibility and emulation ideas that reflect the project’s broader interest in interactive computing.

Roadmap

From website to real playable demos

Our development roadmap begins with a public foundation, progresses through interactive prototypes, and expands into reusable engine systems.

Phase 01

Public Presence

Establish the public project home, repository, open-source licensing, branding, and development foundations.

Phase 02

Momentum Prototype

Create a bouncing-ball downhill track demo to showcase physics, visuals, and engine direction.

Phase 03

Core Engine Expansion

Build reusable systems for scenes, rendering features, audio, tooling, and project exports.
